Merge Rush Privacy Policy

Developer: Khumalo Augustine Games

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Information and local game data

Merge Rush does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. Game progress, high scores, coins, unlocked levels, statistics, onboarding status, and appearance settings are stored locally on your device.

Internet services and advertising

The current release has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, backend server, or cloud database. The game works offline. If these practices change in a future release, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that release is published.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Because Merge Rush does not receive this local data, the developer cannot access or delete it remotely.

Children

Merge Rush is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Keeping game data on the device and avoiding accounts or network transmission reduces exposure. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
